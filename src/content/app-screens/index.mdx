**App & Screens** is the macro half of this wiki: everything above the component
scale, about how a whole app or screen is built, delivered, navigated, and laid
out. Its counterpart, **Building Blocks**, is the micro half: the primitives,
components, data shapes, and patterns a screen is assembled from. This library
reads top-down by scale.

<ScaleMap />

## The sections, by scale

- **Rendering & Delivery** — when a screen becomes visible and when it becomes
  interactive. The rendering choice is really a UX choice about who waits.
- **Routing & Navigation** — where navigation chrome lives, and where view state
  lives so that links, reloads, and the back button all restore the right view.
  This is where web and native diverge hardest.
- **Layouts & Archetypes** — how a single screen is composed: the app shell and
  the recurring whole-screen shapes (master-detail, the nav-rail + triptych,
  dashboards) that most app screens are variations of.

## Why split macro from micro

The same word, "design," covers two very different scales. Picking a rendering
strategy or folding a triptych onto a phone is a different kind of decision from
choosing a button's disabled state or a table's alignment. Keeping the macro
half here and the micro half in <a href="/docs/foundations">Building Blocks</a>
makes that split legible, so a page is about a whole screen or a single
component, never an uneasy mix of both.
